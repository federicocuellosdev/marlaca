Legal structure

Exactly what you buy.

Indonesian law does not allow a foreigner to hold full freehold ownership of land. You therefore buy through a recognised legal structure, which we explain with our notary before you sign. Marlaca runs the villa under a management contract: it rents it out and pays you the net, but it does not own your villa.

Company
PT PMA: an Indonesian foreign-owned company, in your name or shared.
Right
HGB: right to build (Hak Guna Bangunan, HGB) and use the land, for renewable terms.
Support
A notary and local lawyers throughout the process, with documents in English.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us before investing.

What title do I hold over the villa?

You buy through a PT PMA company that holds an HGB right over the land, which allows you to build on and use the property for renewable terms. It is not full ownership of the land, which Indonesian law reserves for its citizens. We explain it with our notary before you sign.

Is the return guaranteed?

No. We work with three scenarios (11%, 14% and 18% a year, before corporate income tax) and show you the assumptions behind each. What we do have are real results from operating villas, such as Villa Nina in 2025.

Can I visit the villas that are already operating?

Yes. If you are in Lombok, we show you operating villas and the construction in progress. If not, we do the visit by video call.

Can I use the villa myself?

Yes. You reserve the dates you want and the villa is rented out the rest of the year. The more nights you use it, the lower the rental result will be.

How much does Marlaca charge to manage the villa?

20% of gross operating profit (GOP): what remains of the income after platform commissions and operating costs.

How do I pay?

40% on signing the sale contract, 40% in milestones during construction and the remaining 20% at least two months before the villa is finished. A fractional share is reserved with €5,000 and, once the Partnership Agreement is signed, the rest is paid. We send you the payment schedule with the contract.

What if I want to invest less than €165,000?

You can get in with a fractional share from €36,800: 4% of the company that owns four 2-bedroom villas at Marlaca IV, with a €5,000 reservation and the rest after signing the Partnership Agreement, and you receive your share of the net profit in a six-monthly distribution. It includes a proportional right of use. No fixed return guaranteed.
