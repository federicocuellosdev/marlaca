Which cookies and similar technologies this website uses, what they are for, and how you can accept them, reject them or change your choice at any time.

Last updated: October 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage (localStorage), serve comparable purposes. They are used to make the website work, to remember your preferences and, if you allow it, to measure how the website is used and how effective our advertising is.

Categories of cookies

We use three categories. On your first visit only the technical category is active; the others only if you authorise them.

Technical (necessary)

Essential to remember your cookie choice. They do not require consent and cannot be switched off.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) tells us, in aggregate, which pages are visited and how the website is used. It is loaded only if you accept it.

Advertising and measurement

Meta Pixel and the Conversions API from Meta Platforms measure how our ads on Facebook and Instagram perform. They include an engagement event after 40 seconds of activity and, if you request a call, the sending of that event together with your contact details (email, phone and name) to attribute the conversion; the Conversions API reaches Meta through a server run by our marketing provider. They are loaded only if you accept them.

You can change your choice at any time with the button below or with the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of any page.
